# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 17 Sep 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to DeCircle Solar ("**DeCircle Solar**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**"), operated by **DeCircle Solar Holdings Pvt Ltd**, a company registered in **India** with its registered office at **602, Lodha Supremus, Powai, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400072**.

DeCircle Solar is an AI-enabled platform for solar project development, finance, and asset transactions, connecting solar developers, investors, EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) contractors, and capital providers across the UAE and India (the "**Platform**" or "**Services**").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when you visit our website at [https://decirclesolar.com](https://decirclesolar.com), create an account, submit a project or investment inquiry, or otherwise interact with our Services (collectively, the "**Site**").

By accessing or using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Site.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide to Us

We may collect the following categories of personal and business information when you interact with us:

- **Contact details:** Full name, email address, phone number, company name, job title.
- **Account information:** Username, password (encrypted), account preferences, if you register on the Platform.
- **Business/professional information:** Company registration details, business type (developer, investor, EPC contractor, capital provider), areas of interest, investment capacity, project specifications, portfolio details.
- **Project and transaction data:** Details of solar projects submitted for development, financing, or sale — including location, capacity (MW), technical specifications, financial projections, ownership structure, and related documentation you upload.
- **Financial information:** Bank or payment details, funding requirements, and investment preferences submitted for due diligence or transaction facilitation purposes. We do not store full payment card details; these are processed via secure third-party payment processors where applicable.
- **Communications:** Messages, inquiries, feedback, or documents you send us through contact forms, live chat, or email.
- **KYC/due diligence documentation:** Identity verification documents, business licenses, and compliance-related records, where required for transactions or regulatory purposes.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect:

- **Device and usage data:** IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, referring URLs, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and click patterns.
- **Location data:** General geographic location inferred from your IP address.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** See Section 6 below.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

- Business partners, EPC contractors, financial institutions, or co-investors involved in a shared transaction.
- Publicly available sources (e.g., company registries, industry databases) for due diligence purposes.
- Analytics and advertising partners (e.g., Google Analytics).

We use the information we collect for purposes including:

1. Operating, maintaining, and improving the Platform and its AI-enabled matching features.
2. Connecting developers, investors, EPC contractors, and capital providers for relevant project opportunities.
3. Verifying your identity and conducting due diligence/KYC checks where required.
4. Facilitating communication and transactions between parties on the Platform.
5. Responding to inquiries, support requests, and customer service needs.
6. Sending administrative communications, updates, and service-related notices.
7. Sending marketing communications and newsletters (where you have opted in, and in accordance with applicable law).
8. Conducting analytics to understand usage patterns and improve user experience.
9. Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraud, security incidents, and misuse of the Platform.
10. Complying with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations in the UAE, India, and other applicable jurisdictions.

**We do not sell your personal information to third parties.**

Where required by applicable data protection law (including the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), we process your information based on one or more of the following:
